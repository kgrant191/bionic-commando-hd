# Area 2 blue slime

Source: `area2-blue-slime-v0113.png`, generated using the built-in image tool.
Prompt: six isolated painted game assets in a 3-column/2-row transparent atlas;
opaque thick cobalt-blue gelatinous slime with navy folds, glossy blue
reflections and a few trapped bubbles. No white spray, foam, watery streaks,
faces, eyes, green or red. Top row: squashed, stretched and curled blob phases.
Bottom row: pooled horizontal strip, thick vertical ribbon, pipe-to-pool join.

The atlas replaces 20 observed background pattern/palette pairs and nine
fluid sprite patterns. Connected native sprite assemblies retain their
original movement and phase timing. Background pools and outlet joins have
separate pieces. Existing Area 2 gates and blue palette matches prevent this
material from replacing future red/green slime palettes in other areas.

The rifleman fix selects two existing painted poses from `enemy-poses-v3.png`;
no new soldier artwork was required. Native head/leg signatures choose each
step; no frame-number animation or gameplay modification is used.
