BIONIC COMMANDO HD — v0.5.0
Cave, combat, generator and Area Clear polish

INSTALL
Load your Bionic Commando (USA).nes ROM in Mesen/MesenCE.
Choose Tools > HD Packs (NES) > Install HD Pack, select this ZIP,
confirm installation, enable HD packs in NES video settings, and reload.

Manual install: put hires.txt and all PNGs together in:
  HdPacks/Bionic Commando (USA)/
The directory name must exactly match your ROM's filename without .nes.

WORK IN PROGRESS
This is a selected-pose pass, not a complete frame-by-frame character redraw.
Unrecognized heads keep the previous material treatment. Occluded recognized
poses use their most common head-matched painting until body signatures return.
Some effects and unrecorded equipment icons still use a smoothed baseline.
Tested against the opening/Area 1, closed-door/hit and cave/generator movies,
plus the earlier controlled menu replay.
The pistol is the starter design; machine-gun/bazooka player art is still pending.
Equipment-dependent graphics are supported by the HD engine, not implemented
for every weapon in this test build.
Later areas and unrecorded states are not validated.
